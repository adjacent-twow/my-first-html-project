There are 7 build archetypes in Dark Souls 3:

Infusions

FromSoftware completely reworked infusion system in Dark Souls 3, making them almost mandatory for any of listed builds. In Dark Souls 3, the infusion rebalances scaling of your weapon, adding ultimate flexibility. You can make any weapon fit for any build. There is a list of all infusions:
